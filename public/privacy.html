Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

This notice covers whiteboard.marcopontili.com, a free browser whiteboard built on Excalidraw. It has no accounts, no login and no backend.

Controller

Marco Pontili.
Contact: marcopontili.com/contact

Your boards stay on your device

Boards, inserted LaTeX and Markdown, and the theme choice are stored in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage) so the app works offline. They are never uploaded. This storage is strictly necessary for the feature you use and is not read by anyone else. Clearing site data in your browser deletes it.

Analytics: self-hosted, cookieless Matomo

  • What: page views, time on page, and anonymous interaction events such as clicks, copies, form and app use, and errors, with an anonymised IP address, browser and device type, referrer and time of visit.
  • How: Matomo runs on the controller's own server. It sets no cookies, builds no cross-site profiles and honours the browser's Do Not Track signal: with DNT on, no visit is recorded. Board contents are never sent. Typed text, form values and file contents are never recorded. Which interactions count is set in a Matomo Tag Manager container on the same self-hosted server; it stores nothing on your device.
  • Why and legal basis: aggregate traffic measurement to keep the app useful, under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). You can object at any time by turning on Do Not Track or through the contact page.
  • Where: European Union (Netsons, Italy). Data is not shared with third parties.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netsons S.r.l. (Italy) as processor. Requests pass through Cloudflare, Inc. as a proxy and cache. Both process connection data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and time, to deliver the site and keep it secure, under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Cloudflare may process this data outside the EU; it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. No Cloudflare analytics run on this site.

Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed for traffic statistics. Server logs follow the hosting provider's standard rotation. Data in your browser stays until you delete it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Use the contact page above. You may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work. The same rights and the same contact route apply wherever you are in the world.
